The Backstory

SALT ROCK TO SURREY

Salties started on the coast of KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa — where the salt air, the surf, and two decades of steel and timber fabrication shaped a way of working that doesn't cut corners.

The name, the logo, the attitude — all of it comes from that place. We carried it to Surrey and built something new with it. The coast is always in the work.
